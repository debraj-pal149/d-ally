Privacy policy

d·ally stores your habits and the days you mark kept or skipped on your phone. If you sign in with Google or Apple, that same information is also stored in Firebase, under your account, so it can appear on your other phones. If you skip sign-in, it stays on that phone only.

Sign-in is handled by Google or Apple. d·ally receives your name, email, and profile photo from the account you choose. A hidden Apple email is stored as given. d·ally does not sell this information and does not show ads.

You can export your history from the Profile tab, sign out, or delete the synced profile. Deleting the profile removes the Firebase copy. The copy on the phone stays until you remove the app.
