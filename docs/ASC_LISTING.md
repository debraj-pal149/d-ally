1) PURPOSE AND AUDIENCE
d·ally is a personal daily-task reminder app for adults who want a simple private rhythm: set everyday tasks at set times, mark them kept / skipped / missed, and see consistency on a calendar. It is not a social network, not a game, and not a business/enterprise tool. Target audience: general consumers (US, India, and other App Store regions). Problem solved: lightweight daily reminders and honest day/week history without streak pressure or a public feed.

2) HOW TO ACCESS / DEMO
No demo account is required. Guest mode works immediately.
1. Launch the app. Finish or skip Welcome.
2. Day tab — tap + to add a daily task (time or finish-by). Mark kept or skip.
3. Calendar tab — see which days were kept; optional Day Bookmarks.
4. On Sunday, “Review your week” may appear from Day (weekly glance).
5. Settings — Privacy Policy, website (dallytasks.app), Terms, Support.
6. Optional: Profile — Sign in with Apple or Google to sync habits across devices. Not required for core use.
7. Account deletion (only if signed in): Profile → delete profile / synced data. Guest users have no server account to delete; local data is removed by deleting the app.

3) EXTERNAL SERVICES (core)
- Apple: Sign in with Apple, push notifications (local scheduling), App Store
- Google: Firebase Authentication, Cloud Firestore (sync when signed in), Google Sign-In
- Mixpanel: product analytics (usage events; not used for advertising tracking)
Privacy policy: https://dallytasks.app/privacy
Support: debraj@theminarnet.com

4) REGIONAL DIFFERENCES
The app functions consistently across all regions where it is available. No region-specific features, content packs, or restricted modes.

5) REGULATED INDUSTRY / PROTECTED MATERIAL
Not applicable. d·ally is a personal productivity / reminder app. It does not operate in a highly regulated industry and does not redistribute protected third-party media libraries.

6) OTHER
No in-app purchases. No user-generated public content, feeds, or messaging — day notes and bookmarks are private to the user. No content reporting/blocking UI is applicable. Free app.
