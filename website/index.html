Your week, in one glance.

On Sunday, Review your week opens the calendar. A moment later, this sheet appears. Close it whenever you want. The calendar stays underneath.

  • What you kept this week, out of what was scheduled.
  • Each day written as kept / scheduled.
  • Last week, only when an earlier week exists.
  • This month so far, and every live task.
